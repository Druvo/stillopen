What you get

Everything the website does, built for a phone

See what is still open

Each job shows whether the posting is still up, how well it fits you and when it closes, so you only spend time on live ones.

A CV that tells the truth

Tailor your CV for a job. Anything that is not in your own material is flagged, and a flagged CV stays locked until you approve it.

Every application in one board

Move jobs through your stages, keep notes, set follow-ups, and get a nudge when a reply is overdue.

Interviews and prep

Keep dates, people and questions for every round, practise a few questions a day, and see your hiring process as a timeline.

Numbers that are honest

Plain counts of what you sent, what got a reply and where you are waiting. Nothing is predicted and small numbers are labelled as such.

You stay in charge

The app never applies for you and never sends anything on its own. It is open source, and you can run your own server.
