Job hunting is tiring. This part doesn't have to be.

Here is what it does for you, in plain terms.

Tells you what is still open

Postings close quietly. We look at each link for you, so you stop spending an evening on a job that is already gone.

Shows how well you fit

Add a job and see, in a sentence and a number, where you are strong and where you are not. Honest, never harsh.

Tailors your CV, truthfully

We reshape your own CV toward each job. Nothing is invented, every version is kept, and you approve it.

Remembers for you

What you sent to whom, and a gentle nudge when it is time to follow up. You always press apply yourself.

Knows where you can work

Remote, on-site or hybrid, in your country or abroad. Tell us once and jobs that don't fit are flagged.

Private to you

Your jobs, CV and notes are yours alone. You can download or delete everything whenever you like.

Works with or without AI

A quick keyword check is built in. Connect your own AI later if you want a more careful read.

Free to start

An email and a password, then three questions. No card, nothing to install.
