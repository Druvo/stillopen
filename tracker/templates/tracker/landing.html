What it does

Everything below works today.

Knows what is still open

Checks each posting and flags it Open, Closed or Unclear. Closed roles you have not applied to move out of your way.

Match % for every role

Turns an evaluation score into a match percentage with colour bands you define.

Applied, pending, closed

Tabs and statuses are yours to rename and reorder. Setting a status stamps the date you applied.

Short summaries and gaps

Each card shows a summary, strengths, gaps and risks, and the next action to take.

Deadlines first

Roles with a deadline inside your chosen window are highlighted and sorted to the top.

Documents with Paperless-ngx

Keep each CV and cover letter next to its role, and send them to your own Paperless-ngx.

Configurable from the admin

Statuses, tabs, score bands, document types, job sources and expiry phrases are all editable. No code changes.

Self-hosted

Your jobs, CVs and notes stay on your machine. Sign in with a password or, optionally, Google.

How it works

From a pile of tabs to one list.

  1. Bring your roles inImport from your career-ops folder, or add them in the admin.
  2. Check what is still openOne click, one admin action, or a scheduled command.
  3. Work the listApply to the strongest open roles, update the status, keep the documents together.
