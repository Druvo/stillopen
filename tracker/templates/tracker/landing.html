How it works

Your first ten minutes

Four steps, nothing to install. Everything in step 2 and 3 happens by itself.

1

Tell us what you want

The roles you are after, where you could work (your country, remote, other countries) and your CV. Three questions, and you can skip them.

2

We search everywhere

One search reads {% if stats %}{{ stats.sources_searched }}{% else %}many{% endif %} company career pages and job boards at once. The first jobs appear in under half a minute.

3

We keep what fits

Jobs outside your places are dropped. The rest are scored against your own skills, best first. No AI is needed, and none is used unless you connect one.

4

You tailor and apply

Get a CV and cover letter shaped for the job, apply on the employer's site yourself, and we track the follow-ups, interviews and offers.
